Rosetta Tone Lab

Wheel Lab

Two ways to fit the tone wheel on a phone. Tap a colour segment to turn the wheel; tap a code to highlight it below. Five brands, one ring each.

  • Clipped wheel: the wheel is wider than the screen and cropped at the sides, so the chosen family (bottom) and its opposite (top) stay visible. Sliders set how far it overflows and its vertical position.
  • Keyhole: a small dial for navigation. The chosen segment grows out of the dial's centre into a large wedge, ring by ring, one per brand, ending with the family name. Tap another segment: the wedge pulls back, the dial turns, and the new wedge grows. Timing sliders tune each step; Slow motion helps judge it.

The detail sheet at the bottom drags up to show every brand's codes for the family, and back down. Tapping its handle toggles it.

On a phone this opens full screen.
